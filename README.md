EcoCash Sentinel
AI-Powered Enterprise Risk Intelligence Platform
EcoCash Sentinel is an integrated Governance, Risk, Compliance and Resilience (GRC) platform designed to replace spreadsheets, disconnected reporting, manual assessments and fragmented governance processes.

Problem
Organizations manage risks, controls, incidents, assessments and reporting in multiple spreadsheets and disconnected systems, resulting in:

Limited visibility of enterprise risks
Slow reporting cycles
Manual board reporting
Poor control monitoring
Difficult compliance tracking
Solution
EcoCash Sentinel provides a single platform for:

Enterprise Risk Management
Risk Assessments
Risk Appetite Monitoring
KRI Monitoring
Incident & Loss Management
Control Library & Testing
Actions & Issues Tracking
Weekly KPI Intelligence
Board Governance & Reporting
Performance Analytics
Audit Trail & MFA Security
AI Capabilities
